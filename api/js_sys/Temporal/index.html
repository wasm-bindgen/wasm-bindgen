PlainDate
A Temporal.PlainDate represents a calendar date without time or time zone information. For example, it could be used to represent an event on a calendar which happens during the whole day no matter which time zone it’s happening in.
PlainDateFields
The date fields accepted by PlainDate.with(), PlainMonthDay.with() and the toPlainDate() methods: era/era_year, year, month/month_code and day, each optional.
PlainDateLike
The property bag accepted by PlainDate.from(): the PlainDateFields plus calendar. from() requires year (or era and era_year), month (or month_code) and day.
PlainDateTime
A Temporal.PlainDateTime represents a calendar date and wall-clock time, with a precision in nanoseconds, and without any time zone.
PlainDateTimeFields
The date and time fields accepted by PlainDateTime.with(), each optional.
PlainDateTimeLike
The property bag accepted by PlainDateTime.from(): the PlainDateTimeFields plus calendar. from() requires year (or era and era_year), month (or month_code) and day. Omitted time fields default to zero.
PlainMonthDay
A Temporal.PlainMonthDay represents a particular day on the calendar, but without a year. For example, it could be used to represent a yearly recurring event, like “Bastille Day is on the 14th of July.”
PlainMonthDayLike
The property bag accepted by PlainMonthDay.from(): the PlainDateFields plus calendar. from() requires day and either month_code, or month together with year in calendars other than ISO 8601.
PlainTime
A Temporal.PlainTime represents a wall-clock time, with a precision in nanoseconds, and without any time zone or calendar information.
PlainTimeLike
The time fields accepted by PlainTime.from() and with(), each optional. from() requires at least one of them.
PlainYearMonth
A Temporal.PlainYearMonth represents a particular month on the calendar. For example, it could be used to represent a particular instance of a monthly recurring event, like “the June 2019 meeting”.
PlainYearMonthFields
The fields accepted by PlainYearMonth.with(): era/era_year, year and month/month_code, each optional.
PlainYearMonthLike
The property bag accepted by PlainYearMonth.from(): the PlainYearMonthFields plus calendar. from() requires year (or era and era_year) and month (or month_code).
